# Vision

## Purpose

This project is a knowledge harness. It turns scoped activity into evidence-bearing,
reviewable, retrievable knowledge without owning that knowledge.

Agent-driven work produces useful records that are otherwise transient: the same
underlying facts often need different interpretations and presentations for different
audiences. The harness makes knowledge growth predictable and inspectable while leaving
ownership, storage, and release policy with the knowledge space itself.

The harness provides the universal mechanics of capture, reconciliation, approval, and
presentation. Reusable knowledge packs supply domain-specific judgment. Independent
knowledge spaces own their private durable state. The harness does not claim to solve
knowledge management generally.

## Governing principles

### 1. Independent knowledge spaces

The harness operates over many independent knowledge spaces. Each space is a hard
ownership, storage, privacy, and authorization boundary. Supporting multiple spaces is
necessary for ownership and portability; it is not a commitment to a hosted or shared
service.

### 2. Provenance to durable records

Evidence and claims must remain traceable to their sources, scope, and collection
context. Claims, interpretations, decisions, and recommendations remain distinguishable
from the evidence behind them. Derived and audience-shaped output never becomes new
evidence by repetition.

### 3. Deliberate approval

The harness may automate extraction, comparison, and proposal. It does not promote
model-generated statements into active knowledge without the applicable approval, and
every candidate record remains inactive until that disposition is applied.
Non-additive changes and broader generalization are explicit, visible transactions.

### 4. Durable human-readable records

Durable evidence and knowledge are stored as human-readable records owned by the space.
All derived state — indexes and materialized views — is disposable and can be rebuilt
from the authorized corpus. Durable state remains inspectable without the harness.

### 5. Host and pack separation

A host environment provides the execution substrate: an agent loop, session behavior,
and provider integration. A knowledge pack supplies reusable domain judgment: candidate
kinds, source validity, evidence grading, scope meaning, and view guidance. Neither is
owned by the harness, and neither reimplements the universal protocol.

### 6. Privacy by ownership

Private knowledge never becomes a harness fixture, release payload, or shared artifact.
All space-owned private content remains inside its owning space. Cross-space retrieval
and promotion are closed by default.

### 7. Immutable release use

A consuming environment uses a versioned, immutable release and does not change
installed behavior in place. Space-owned knowledge is a runtime input, not a release
content.

### 8. Simplicity

Every schema field, abstraction, configuration surface, and compatibility promise
creates ongoing cost. Prefer visible records, explicit transactions, and deletion. Do
not build a database, workflow graph, ontology, or federation layer before an observed
behavior requires one.

## Non-goals

The project does not aim to:

- determine objective truth or replace human and domain judgment;
- ingest raw sessions directly into a durable corpus;
- promote claims or generalize across spaces autonomously;
- operate as a hosted service, central knowledge lake, or shared platform;
- make a derived retrieval layer an authorization boundary;
- replace the host, domain tools, source repositories, or record systems it works with;
- resolve every knowledge-management problem, or serve every audience.
